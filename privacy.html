Privacy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Who we are

This website belongs to SWK Studios LLC, a Minnesota company. This page explains what happens to your information when you visit it.

What we collect

Nothing. The site has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no cookies and no forms. We do not build visitor profiles and we do not sell or share personal data.

All fonts, images and code are served from this website. Your browser does not contact any other service when the page loads.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, and may keep it in server logs for security and reliability. GitHub handles that information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use those logs.

Contacting us

You can contact us through our LinkedIn company page. Messages you send there are handled by LinkedIn under its own privacy policy. We use what you send only to reply to you and to discuss the work you ask about, and we do not add you to any mailing list.

Links to other sites

This site links to LinkedIn and GitHub. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which apply once you follow a link.

Changes and questions

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. For any question about privacy, message us on LinkedIn.
